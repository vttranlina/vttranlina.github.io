Highlights

Production and reliability

  • Side-service and Sabre 2.4.5.1 were deployed to LNG production (side-service release comment, Sabre release comment).
  • The post-deployment OpenSearch reindex failed after processing 153,325 events, with four failed events. The report does not show whether those failures were retried or resolved (issues/1018 result).
  • Distinct DAV client User-Agent headers were introduced to improve traffic identification and diagnostics (pull/1022).

Common Contacts

  • Outbound Common Contacts publishing was merged. Review established that this should be a platform capability rather than requiring the full SaaS extension, and prompted extraction of reusable RabbitMQ consumer lifecycle support (pull/1013 review comment, pull/1020).
  • Inbound collection remains open. Because neither Sabre nor current side-service libraries provide vCard merge support, the accepted direction is to retrieve an existing contact, merge it, and PUT the result back (pull/1017 merge discussion).
  • Follow-up work was opened for documentation and bulk contact republishing (issues/1021, issues/1024).

Team Calendar

  • Sabre now preserves team-calendar metadata when an event is moved from a personal calendar, with integration tests covering event moves and attendee participation updates (pull/460, pull/325).
  • Frontend event previews now display team organizers consistently for team members and non-members (pull/1264).
  • The open participation-link defect requires resource-calendar detection and technical-token access; implementation still needs a way to locate the resource event by UID or carry its href in the JWT (issues/1023 analysis).

Booking and event presentation

  • Public booking creation and display gained expanded fields and extra-attendee support across frontend and side-service (pull/1254, pull/1255, pull/1012, pull/1260).
  • Organizer-decline notifications to bookers were fixed in side-service. Related integration coverage is approved, but pull/307 was not reported as merged (pull/1019).
  • Basic rich-text rendering was merged with a restrictive allowlist and safer parser. Rich-text event creation remains open and intentionally separate (pull/1259, issues/1256).
